## Feature of the week

**Tone before voice.** Spoken alerts now start with a short tone, so your coms are awake before the words arrive.

Cardo and Sena units clip the first half second of phone audio while the link wakes up. That half second used to be the first word of the alert. Now a tone takes the hit and the words land clean. Three shapes, so you know which call it is before anyone speaks: rising for bird up, three quick for bird near, falling for all clear. Turn on Voice readback in [Alerts](/settings/alerts#voice) and Tone first comes on with it. The three buttons play each tone, so you can learn them in the garage with the helmet on. Tones are generated on the phone, nothing to download. Voice still needs the page open; phone locked, use Telegram.
