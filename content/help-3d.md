### 3D view

Tap **3D** above the map on a flight page to see the track over real terrain, at the height the bird reported. A faint curtain drops from the path to the ground so altitude reads at a glance. Terrain and altitude share the same 1.3× vertical stretch, so the gap between path and ground stays honest.

- **Replay.** Play runs the whole flight in under a minute. Drag the bar to scrub. The readout shows the time (PT), altitude and ground speed at that moment.
- **Patterns.** Where the bird circled one stretch of road, a white ring marks the pattern at the height it was flown, with the corridor and how long it stayed. Tap the pattern chip under the map to swing the camera there.
- **Moving around.** Pinch to zoom, twist to rotate, two-finger drag to tilt. **Reset view** puts the camera back.
- **Sharing.** A link opened in 3D stays in 3D (`?view=3d`).

With reduced motion turned on, nothing plays or swoops on its own. Browsers without WebGL2 get the flat map and a short note.
