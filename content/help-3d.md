### 3D view

Tap **3D** above the map on a flight page to see the track over real terrain, at the height the bird reported. A faint curtain drops from the path to the ground so altitude reads at a glance. Terrain and altitude share the same 1.3× vertical stretch, so the gap between path and ground stays honest.

- **Replay.** Play runs the whole flight in under a minute. Drag the bar to scrub. The readout shows the time (PT), altitude and ground speed at that moment.
- **Patterns.** Where the bird circled one stretch of road, a white ring marks the pattern at the height it was flown, with the corridor and how long it stayed. Tap the pattern chip under the map to swing the camera there.
- **Moving around.** Pinch to zoom, twist two fingers to rotate, drag two fingers up or down to tilt. With a mouse, right-drag or Ctrl-drag to rotate and Shift-drag to tilt. **Reset view** puts the camera back.
- **Getting another angle.** The buttons under Reset view turn the camera 45° left or right and tilt it a step at a time, from straight down to low over the horizon. The compass shows which way is north; tap it to put north back up. **Orbit** spins the view slowly around the middle of the map. It only starts when you tap it, and any drag, pinch, scroll or button stops it.
- **Keyboard.** Click the map first, then Q and E rotate, W and S tilt.
- **Sharing.** A link opened in 3D stays in 3D (`?view=3d`).

With reduced motion turned on, nothing plays or swoops on its own, the camera buttons jump instead of gliding, and Orbit is not offered. Browsers without WebGL2 get the flat map and a short note.

The roads under the track take a muted tint from your radar palette, so you can tell the interstate from the side streets without the roads outshouting the flight.
