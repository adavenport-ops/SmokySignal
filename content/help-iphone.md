# iPhone setup

Three switches decide whether SmokySignal works on an iPhone: the home screen, location and notifications. Do them in this order. It takes about two minutes, and you only do it once.

## 1. Add it to the home screen

iPhone only sends alerts to a web app that lives on the home screen, and location behaves better there too.

1. Open **smokysignal.app** in Safari.
2. Tap **Share** (the square with the arrow). On iOS 26 it sits under the **⋯** button.
3. Scroll down and tap **Add to Home Screen**. If you see **Open as Web App**, leave it on.
4. Tap **Add**.

From now on, open SmokySignal from the new icon, not from a Safari tab. Alerts need iOS 16.4 or later.

## 2. Turn on location

Location is how the radar puts you on the map and how the speed warning knows the bird is close. It never leaves your phone.

1. Open the iPhone **Settings** app, then **Privacy & Security** › **Location Services**. Make sure it is on.
2. Scroll down the list to **Safari Websites**. Pick **While Using the App** and turn on **Precise Location**.
3. Open SmokySignal from the home-screen icon, go to **Radar**, and tap **Allow** when it asks.

Still blocked? You may have tapped **Don't Allow** once. Go to **Settings** › **Apps** › **Safari** › **Location** (on iOS 17 and earlier, **Settings** › **Safari** › **Location**) and set it to **Ask** or **Allow**, then reopen SmokySignal.

## 3. Allow notifications

1. In SmokySignal, open [Settings › Alerts](/settings/alerts) and tap **Arm alerts**.
2. Tap **Allow** when iPhone asks.
3. Check it stuck: iPhone **Settings** › **Notifications** › **SmokySignal**. **Allow Notifications** on, with **Lock Screen** and **Banners** ticked.

No prompt at all? You are in a Safari tab, not the home-screen app. Go back to step 1.

A Focus mode can hold alerts back. In **Settings** › **Focus**, open the Focus you ride with (Driving, for one), then **Apps**, and add SmokySignal.

## 4. Voice readback

Voice reads each alert aloud, which is handy with helmet coms.

1. In SmokySignal, open [Settings › Alerts](/settings/alerts#voice) and tick **Enabled** under **Voice**.
2. Pair your coms before you ride and turn the volume up.

Voice only speaks while SmokySignal is open on screen. With the phone locked you still get the notification, but nothing is read aloud.

## Quick check

- The icon is on your home screen and you opened SmokySignal from it.
- **Radar** shows your dot, a sky-blue circle.
- [Settings › Alerts](/settings/alerts) shows **Disarm**, which means you are armed, and **Send me a test ping** arrives.
