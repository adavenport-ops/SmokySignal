## Product vision (creative, design and three persona passes)

### Who it's for

| Persona | Arrives via | Core question | Today |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Rider** (Marcus, Tacoma→Bellevue commuter, weekend group-ride organizer) | home-screen PWA, push | "Is it on *my road*, and which way is it heading?" | Answers "anywhere in WA". No corridor, heading, or distance. |
| **Curious local** (Dana, Puyallup, non-rider) | a shared `/plane` or `/flight` link from Facebook | "What is that plane circling my street, and is this legit?" | Lands on jargon ("bird", "Send it."), sees rider-only prompts, and the share link was broken (#134). |
| **Aviation enthusiast** (Priya, PPL, runs her own ADS-B receiver) | Reddit/Discord | "How does the unit actually fly?" Orbits, loiter, bases, airframes. | Impressed by the registry and the per-plane heatmap. Would publicly call out the dropout "takeoffs", an unspecified altitude datum, and unsourced airframe data. |

### What all three passes agree on

1. **Truth comes first.** ADS-B dropouts appear as takeoffs and landings (TROY 169 cycling about 20 times in one afternoon). This pollutes /activity, the forecast's "260 takeoffs", and flight durations. → fix/data-integrity (in flight); then a visible **"lost contact"** state that is distinct from "down".
2. **"Smokey" vs everything else.** The fleet mixes WSP, sheriff, CBP and USCG airframes. The heatmap is dominated by King County Sheriff (KCSO) helicopters out of Renton (KRNT), yet the copy implies WSP speed enforcement. → Show agency and mission labels everywhere. Add a default "Speed enforcement only / Everything" filter, and apply it to the heatmap and forecast as well.
3. **"Send it." on the all-clear state** reads as "go fast now" to non-riders, troopers, and skeptics. It contradicts `/help` ("Ride within the limit"). **Brand decision for Alex.** Recommendation: retire it from the hero and from spoken copy.
4. **Prompts crowd out the answer.** On mobile, the fixed "iOS push: 2 steps" bar covers ~80px of every route, including the radar controls. "ALERTS BLOCKED" (with Safari wording on Android) shows before the rider has tapped anything. → One slim, dismissible inline card, never on /radar; show permission state only after the rider taps Arm.
5. **Location context.** "Near me", distance and bearing in the "also up" rows (currently a dead "—"), heading, and corridor names.
6. **History is evaporating.** Tracks and activity have a 35-day TTL, so every idea about the past needs a permanent daily rollup. **Start it now**; it's small.

### Design direction

- **One "up" colour.** The hero, chips and CTAs render amber `#F5B840`, while BRAND says `--alert #FF7A1A` means only "Smokey is up". Make orange exclusive to "up". Move the heatmap to a cool ramp so aircraft are the only warm thing on the map.
- **Type scale** 56/28/18/14/11 with tabular-nums. Metadata minimum 12px at fg-1 (fg-2 at 10–11px fails in sunlight). Every control at least 48px for gloved use.
- **Desktop is a stretched phone.** At ≥1280px, use an **ops console**: fleet roster with uptime sparklines, a central live map, and a mono radio-log event ticker, with a 24h up/down timeline strip along the bottom.
- **Signature moments:** a "bird up" takeover (full-bleed orange edge glow, 96px readout, compass needle pointing at the bird); a slow CB-radio radar sweep, so the idle map feels alive; hollow grey last-known glyphs when nothing is up.
- **One glyph set** that distinguishes fixed-wing from helicopter, rotated to heading. Replace the drone-like radar button with a locate icon.

### Feature roadmap (merged and ranked)

**Now: truth and trust (S each)**
- Dropout debounce and a "lost contact" state *(in flight)*. Permanent daily flight rollup.
- Agency/mission labels plus a "Speed enforcement only" default filter.
- Registry provenance: confirmed/tentative badge, source per field, altitude datum stated (barometric).
- Retire "Send it."; a plain "What is this?" strip on first visit (not affiliated with WSP; public ADS-B is legal to receive).
- Prompt clean-up (install bar, alerts-blocked wording per platform).

**Next: rider value (S–M)**
- Distance, bearing and heading in the hero and "also up" rows; a "moving toward / away" cue.
- **Corridors:** `lib/corridor.ts` (I-5, I-405, SR-167, SR-512, SR-410, US-2, I-90). Labels in /activity (#73), corridor presets in /settings/zones, a corridor filter on /forecast ("best windows Sunday"), and corridor-scoped push.
- **Status share card** (timestamped OG image) plus a one-tap share on the hero, for the group chat.
- **Relay:** a Discord/Slack webhook per club, reusing the dispatcher's dedupe and quiet-hours logic.

**Then: curious locals and aviation enthusiasts (M)**
- **What's Circling?** An orbit detector over `TrackPoint.trk`: "Working a pattern over SR-512 for 22 min, out of Olympia." Tags on /radar and /plane; neighbourhood/road pages (`/road/sr-512`).
- **Airport matching** for takeoff and landing (KOLM → KTIW). A per-airframe logbook (sorties, hours/month, time on station, altitude bands).
- **Capture more per point:** squawk, MLAT flag, baro vs geometric altitude, vertical rate. Prerequisite for any coverage claims.
- **Coverage honesty and Raise an Antenna:** a dropout-derived blind-spot layer, plus a how-to guide for feeding adsb.fi.
- **Flight card export** (PNG: map, altitude/speed profile, orbit stats).
- **/program explainer:** agencies, airframes, what FLIR does, links to public WSP sources; "we don't know" where unsourced.

**Later: culture and distribution (M–L)**
- **The Board:** `/board` split-flap departure kiosk for moto shops and cafés.
- **The Beacon:** `/api/status.txt` (up/down/stale) plus Home Assistant snippet and DIY ESP32 light.
- **Channel 19 Hotline:** call or text a number and hear the status, with nothing logged (Twilio; voice is safer than a glance at the phone).
- **Open Ledger:** monthly flight hours per airframe as CC-BY CSV, coarse geography (check adsb.fi redistribution terms).
- **Season Wrap:** end-of-season recap cards, plus an optional printed zine as a tip-jar path.
- /stats, ride check (route kept on the device), handlebar `/glance`, PWA offline shell, weather line, a11y v2 (from the first pass).

**Deliberately not doing:** endurance/"lands in ~40 min" countdowns (evasion timing), ride debriefs, crew or shift rosters, citation-record overlays, a morning-brief push, collectible tail stamps, or OR/ID expansion before registry accuracy is solid.

## Phase 2 — Decisions needed (Alex)

1. **Snapshot cadence.** `vercel.json` runs `refresh-snapshot` once daily (Hobby). Tracks,
   activity and takeoff pushes only happen when a rider's request misses the 10s cache.
   Roadmap assumes 60s. Options: Vercel Pro per-minute cron · GitHub Actions every 5 min ·
   external pinger (Mac Mini / helm) every 60s.
2. **Existing `dryrun-warnings:*` keys in prod KV** contain rider lat/lon/speed. Delete?
3. **Server-side zones & spot GPS** (push prefs, `/api/spot`) contradict the privacy posture
   in CLAUDE.md. Coarsen to ~1 km, or move matching client-side, or amend the docs.
4. **`?mock=` in production** lets anyone share a fake "all clear" link. Gate to non-prod?
5. **OpenSky fallback** is failing auth — rotate the credential or drop the fallback.
6. **Heartbeat** — KV secrets are likely missing from GitHub Actions; re-add or retire.
