# SmokySignal plan, November 2026 to June 2027

Set 2026-11-19. Detail lives in `docs/BACKLOG.md` (items B20 onward); this is
the short version and the reasoning. Research to date:
`docs/research/us-police-aircraft-2026-11.md`.

## The goal in one line

By Motorcycle Safety Awareness Month (May 2027), SmokySignal is the best
public record of police aircraft in the United States, and the place any
rider in any state checks before a ride.

## Who it is for, and what each wants

| Persona | Wants | What we build for them |
|---|---|---|
| Sport-bike rider (primary) | Is the bird up near me, now, without fiddling | Ride mode, earcon and spoken callouts, banner speed warning, range setting |
| Commuter | Same road daily, quiet unless it matters | Corridor alerts, lock-screen copy that names the road |
| Group-ride leader | One heads-up for the group, no nagging | "Got it" per pass, share status link, Telegram group alerts |
| Tourer / road-tripper | Crossing states; what flies where I am going | National tracking, state pages, route check across states |
| Curious local | "What is that plane circling over my town" | /seen, /near/[town], /circling, plain answers |
| Aviation fan | Tails, types, bases, histories, data | Per-aircraft dossiers, agency pages, national map, exports |
| Journalist / researcher | Sourced facts, counts, trends | Agency pages with sources, monthly data report, press kit, CSV |
| Civil-liberties watcher | Who flies over whom, how often, drones | Activity by agency, drone program list, method notes |
| Off-duty trooper | Fair, accurate, not anti-enforcement | Neutral copy, sources on every fact, corrections path |
| Low-vision rider | Readable in sun, at speed | Contrast, sizes, voice (already covered by the a11y track) |

The thread through all of them: accurate, sourced, current. That is also what
search engines and AI answers reward, so the database is the product and the
marketing at once.

## Track 1: the national database

Every police aircraft in the country (state patrol, sheriff, city police, and
federal law enforcement as its own class), each with a researched record.

1. **Registry pipeline per state** (B31). FAA registry by owner name, N-number
   to transponder code, mission class, a source per row.
2. **Track by tail, nationwide** (B30, B34b). Ask the feeds for our list of
   transponder codes instead of one circle around Washington. Every state at
   once, with no extra map regions to maintain.
3. **Activity status** (B39). An aircraft is shown only while it is active:
   - When first found, it counts as active only if it flew in the last 30 days.
   - Any flight makes it active again at once.
   - With no flight for 60 days it is hidden from lists, alerts and the
     speed warning. Its record and history stay, and its page says
     "last flew <date>".
4. **Dossiers** (B40). For each active aircraft: agency, unit, base airport
   (from its own takeoffs), type and year, registration date, flights and
   hours per month, typical roads and hours, notable public facts with
   sources (agency pages, budget documents, news). Facts about the aircraft
   and the agency only, never about individual crew.
5. **Agency pages** (B42). One page per agency: fleet, activity, where it
   flies, whether it runs aerial speed enforcement, sources.
6. **Drones** (B41). Police drones rarely broadcast ADS-B, and Remote ID is
   local radio, so there is no national live feed. What we can publish is
   which agencies run drone programs, from public data: EFF's Atlas of
   Surveillance (CC BY), FAA waiver and authorization lists, city council
   records and news. That goes on agency pages as "drone program: yes, since
   <year>, source". Live drone tracking is out of scope unless a public feed
   appears.

## Track 2: hear it on the bike

The web app can only speak while the page is open, so both cases get a path:
- **Phone on the bars, unlocked:** ride mode (B25), earcons (B23), spoken
  callouts (B26), the banner warning with "Got it" (shipped #192).
- **Phone locked:** better push copy (B24) and Telegram (B27), which iPhone
  and Android Auto can read aloud.

"Every state" means every state with police aircraft that broadcast. A state
with no active aircraft gets a page that says so and lists what it has, which
is itself an answer people search for.

## Track 4: marketing to June

Constraint so far: no new accounts without Alex; every outbound post is
approved by Alex before it goes.

| Month | Riders | Aviation fans and locals | Press and search |
|---|---|---|---|
| Nov to Dec | Facebook riding groups (Muse handoff), reply to feedback, ship what riders ask for and say so | r/ADSB post on the archive backfill and the open data | Weekly blog; IndexNow; town pages |
| January | Ride mode demo clip (Shorts, Reels); riding-club newsletters; poster at shops | ADS-B forums: the tail-based national tracker | Pitch: "the busiest patrol plane in Washington", from our data |
| February | State riding groups in OR and ID ahead of launch | Agency and aircraft pages indexed; llms.txt facts | Data story: how many police aircraft fly in the US, sourced |
| March | OR and ID launch posts; dealer and gear-shop posters | Spotter communities: per-aircraft pages | Local TV and radio in OR and ID with the launch |
| April | Rider-safety orgs and track-day groups in launch states | Monthly data report | State-by-state SEO pages live |
| May | National launch, one post per state riding group over the month | Show HN, r/dataisbeautiful with the national map | Press release for Motorcycle Safety Awareness Month; journalist list per state |
| June | Season feedback round; testimonials (with permission) on the site | | Mid-season data report |

Message by persona: riders hear "know what is overhead, ride within the limit";
locals hear "what is that plane over my town"; aviation fans hear "every police
aircraft in the US, live and sourced"; press hears "the first public national
record of police aviation activity".

Measures: weekly visitors, push and Telegram subscribers, pages indexed,
searches answered (Search Console), and feedback items closed.

## Not doing

- Showing aircraft as "Smokey" unless the mission is police.
- Anything that reads as help evading enforcement.
- Personal data about crews, or storing rider location server-side.
- A native app before ride mode and Telegram are measured.
