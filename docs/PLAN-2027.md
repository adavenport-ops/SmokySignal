# SmokySignal plan, November 2026 to June 2027

Set 2026-11-19, rewritten the same day to be three times as ambitious.
Detailed, build-ready items live in `docs/BACKLOG.md`. This file holds the
themes and every planned feature as a one-line **P-item**. The weekly job turns
P-items into full backlog items as the ranked list runs low (see "How this
file is used" at the end).

Research to date: `docs/research/us-police-aircraft-2026-11.md`.

## Where this goes

By May 2027 (Motorcycle Safety Awareness Month), SmokySignal is:

1. **The national record of police aviation.** Every police aircraft in the
   US, live, with a researched page, an activity status and sources.
2. **The thing riders hear, not just look at.** In the helmet, on the
   watch, on the dash, with Siri, with the phone locked.
3. **An AI-native data source.** Ask it in plain English on the site, in
   Claude or ChatGPT (MCP), with Siri, or in Telegram, and get a sourced
   answer.
4. **A data showcase people share.** Replays, wraps, maps and reports that
   travel on their own.

## Themes

### T1 · National record (the database)

- P01 Tail-based tracking nationwide (feeds asked for our transponder codes, not a circle)
- P02 State registry pipeline from the FAA registry, every state, a source per row
- P03 Activity rule: active if flown in 30 days at discovery; any flight reactivates; hidden after 60 days idle; page stays with "last flew"
- P04 Aircraft dossiers: agency, base, type, year, hours, usual roads, sourced facts
- P05 AI research agent per tail: drafts the dossier with citations, a person approves (review queue page in /admin)
- P06 Agency pages with fleet, activity, aerial-enforcement status, drone program
- P07 Drone programs by agency (EFF Atlas of Surveillance, FAA waivers, council records)
- P08 Type pages ("every police Cessna 206 in the US") and base-airport pages ("police aircraft based at KOLM")
- P09 New-tail discovery: unknown aircraft that fly enforcement-like patterns get flagged for research
- P10 Fleet changes feed: new aircraft, retired, sold, re-registered (FAA registry diff, monthly)
- P11 Federal law enforcement class (CBP, FBI, DEA, USMS) shown separately, never as Smokey
- P12 Corrections path on every record: "Something wrong" opens a prefilled email, logged publicly when fixed

### T2 · Hear it on the bike

- P13 Ride mode: one tap, screen awake, voice on, glance layout
- P14 Earcons before voice so helmet coms wake up
- P15 Spoken callouts: "Smokey 4, 3 miles north, closing", and "clear"
- P16 Siri Shortcut: "Hey Siri, is Smokey up" answered through the helmet, phone locked (Shortcuts runs a URL and speaks the reply)
- P17 Android: an Assistant routine and a home-screen widget link doing the same
- P18 Telegram live, with Announce Notifications and Android Auto guide
- P19 Lock-screen push copy that names who, how far, which road, updated in place
- P20 Proximity tiers: heads-up and close, each with its own sound
- P21 Corridor alerts nationally
- P22 Morning ride brief: one push or Telegram message at a chosen time, "Quiet so far; Saturdays on SR-410 usually see one pass after 10:00"
- P23 Speed-warning range setting and "Got it" per pass (shipped the latter in #192)
- P24 Watch surface: a PWA glance tuned for Apple Watch-size Safari and Wear OS browsers, plus Telegram on the watch

### T3 · Ask Smokey (the AI project)

- P25 SmokySignal MCP server: a public, read-only remote MCP endpoint (`/mcp`) with tools `status`, `aircraft`, `flights`, `what_flew_over`, `agency`, `state_summary`, `forecast`. Listed in the MCP registries, so anyone can ask Claude "is the patrol plane up on I-5"
- P26 Ask Smokey chat on the site: a small chatbot with a CB-radio voice, answering only from our own tools, linking every answer to the page that proves it; Haiku-class model, hard daily cost cap, no accounts, no storage of questions beyond aggregate counts
- P27 Plain-English search across the site ("sheriff helicopter over Bellevue last month at night") built on the same tools
- P28 Flight narratives: every flight gets a short written account from its track ("took off from Olympia 07:12, worked I-5 between Tumwater and Centralia for 1 h 40 min in 11 loops"). Template first, a model polishes; shown on flight pages and indexed
- P29 Pattern detector: classify each flight segment as orbit, racetrack over a road, line patrol, grid survey, transit; "working I-5 between mileposts 95 and 101" on the live view
- P30 Mission guesser for unknown tails from flight patterns, shown as "looks like survey work" with a confidence, never as fact
- P31 Anomaly watch: unusual activity (long night orbit, many aircraft converging) drafts a "what was circling X last night" post for a person to check and publish
- P32 Smokey forecast model: probability of a patrol pass per corridor per hour, from history, day, season and weather; honest error bars
- P33 Weekly AI digest: a two-paragraph spoken summary of the week per state, for the blog, Telegram and a podcast-style audio file
- P34 OpenAPI spec for the public API so GPT actions and agent frameworks can use it too
- P35 Eval set: 200 real questions with known answers, run on every change to Ask Smokey and the MCP server

### T4 · Show the data

- P36 National live map with every active police aircraft
- P37 Replay theatre: any day, any state, time-lapse in 2D or 3D, shareable link and auto-rendered clip
- P38 Smokey Wrapped per state and per aircraft, yearly and per season, as share cards
- P39 Calendar heat grids per aircraft and per agency (a year at a glance)
- P40 Leaderboards: busiest aircraft, longest flight, most-worked corridor, per state and national, updated weekly
- P41 Corridor pages for every interstate and major state route, with patrol presence by hour and day
- P42 Patrol presence by hour along a route the rider draws or uploads (GPX stays on the phone)
- P43 "Then and now": how a state's air patrol changed year on year
- P44 Sky sounds: a gentle sonification of the day's traffic for the replay (fun, optional)
- P45 Printable posters per state and per corridor (extends the shop poster)
- P46 Embeds everywhere: club sites, Discord, Twitch overlays, OBS browser source
- P47 Monthly data report and open data release (CSV and Parquet, ODbL attribution)
- P48 Public notebooks (Observable) that reproduce the monthly numbers

### T5 · Rider tools

- P49 Ride planner: pick a route and a day, see patrol presence, weather and daylight together
- P50 Group link: a leader shares one link; everyone sees the same status and the same alerts
- P51 Ride check (existing B08) across states
- P52 Commute card: my road, my hours, how often it is worked
- P53 Weather line and ride window (existing B03, extended with daylight)
- P54 Spotter log: tap "I see it" to confirm a sighting; aggregated, never located finer than a town
- P55 Local-only achievements for spotters ("seen 5 different tails"), nothing stored server-side
- P56 Track-day and event mode: quiet alerts inside a track's bounds

### T6 · Civic and transparency

- P57 Agency transparency notes: does the agency publish flight logs, a drone policy, an annual report; sourced, neutral
- P58 Public-records request helper: a filled-in template per agency citing dates we observed, for people who want to ask
- P59 "Why do they fly" explainers per mission type, written with input from the off-duty-trooper persona
- P60 Methods page: how we identify, what we cannot see (LADD, PIA, gaps), and error rates

### T7 · Devices and integrations

- P63 Stream Deck and LaMetric recipes

- P65 Bluesky autopost per state (one account, state hashtags) once Alex approves the account

### T8 · Platform

## How this file is used

- Three helm runs a week: **Wednesday** (one feature, SEO refresh, weekly
  blog post), **Saturday** (up to two small features, no post), **Monday**
  (data and research: registries, dossiers, drone records, plus one small
  item). Each run picks from `docs/BACKLOG.md` as before.
- When fewer than six unblocked one-run items remain in the ranked list, the
  run expands the next P-items for the current month into full backlog
  items (id, Why, Size, Acceptance, Blog line) and ranks them.
- Big items (MCP server, Ask Smokey, national tracking) are split into
  one-run slices first; nothing ships half-wired behind a visible button.
- AI features need an Anthropic API key with a spend limit set outside the
  code; until Alex adds one, those items stay **Blocked** and their
  non-model parts (tools, templates, evals) ship first.

## Rules that do not move

- Smokey means police; nothing helps anyone evade enforcement.
- No rider location or speed leaves the device; no crew personal data.
- Sources on every fact; corrections are public.
- Every outbound post or message to people is approved by Alex.
