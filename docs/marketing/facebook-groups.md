# Facebook riding groups: launch post and one-month follow-up

Two posts for the riding groups Alex is in. Post A goes out once per group,
staggered over the week of 2026-11-19 (one group a day at most, never two
groups in the same hour). Post B goes out once per group around
2026-12-19, refreshed with that month's numbers.

Rules for every post:

- Alex approves the final text before anything is posted. Nothing is sent
  on a schedule he has not seen.
- Read each group's rules first. Skip a group that bans self-promotion or
  links, or ask the admin.
- Use a different variant in groups that overlap. Facebook flags identical
  text posted across groups as spam.
- One link only, and say plainly that Alex built it.
- Never anything that reads as "speed when it is down". The pitch is
  knowing what is overhead.
- Answer comments by hand. No auto-replies.
