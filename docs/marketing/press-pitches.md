# Press pitches

Short emails. Contacts: find the tips address on each outlet's site, or the
reporter who covers transportation, public safety or technology. Do not
guess addresses. Send from your own mail, one outlet at a time, and attach
nothing large: link the press kit.

Lead with the dataset. It is the story: nobody publishes flight hours for
these aircraft, and it is open, sourced and caveated.

Press kit: https://www.smokysignal.app/press
Dataset: https://www.smokysignal.app/data
Refresh the numbers before sending: 258 flights, 45.6 hours aloft, 12
aircraft flying in the 30 days to 18 Nov 2026.

Never pitch it as a way to avoid tickets. The angles are transparency,
public records and open data.

## Template

**Subject:** [angle for this outlet]

> Hi [name],
>
> I built an open dataset of flight hours for the Washington State Patrol
> and county sheriff aircraft that fly speed enforcement, from public ADS-B.
> [One outlet-specific line.]
>
> In the last 30 days the tracked patrol aircraft flew 258 flights and 45.6
> hours. The monthly figures by aircraft and by mission are a free CSV
> (ODbL 1.0) at smokysignal.app/data, with the method and its limits stated.
> Each aircraft in the registry lists its sources (FAA records, agency pages,
> local reporting). There are no accounts and no rider location is stored.
>
> Logos, screenshots, a 3D clip and the citation line are at
> smokysignal.app/press. I am happy to walk through the method or run a
> number you want to check. I built it as a hobby; it is not affiliated with
> any agency.
>
> [Name]

## After sending

- No follow-up before five working days. One follow-up, one line, no
  attachment.
- If a reporter asks for a number, give the query: the CSV column and the
  month, so they can reproduce it.
