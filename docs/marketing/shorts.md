# Shorts, Reels and TikTok captions

Use the weekly clips under `public/blog/media/<slug>/` (MP4, 6 to 8 seconds,
muted). `npm run marketing:social -- --post <slug>` writes two ready captions
into `docs/marketing/weekly/<slug>.md`; these templates are for hand edits.

Rules for every platform: say what the clip shows, give one number, end with
the site. No emoji, no exclamation marks, no "evade" or "dodge". Add burned-in
captions: most viewers watch muted. Disclose nothing is a forecast.

Hashtags: #Seattle #PNW #ADSB, plus one of #motorcycle or #aviation. Keep
them to four.

## Platform notes

| Platform | Length | Caption limit | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| YouTube Shorts | 60 s max, use 6 to 8 | 100 characters in the title, more in the description | Put the hashtags in the description |
| Instagram Reels | 90 s max | 2,200 | First line shows before "more" |
| TikTok | 10 min max, use 6 to 8 | 4,000 | Keep it a single clean loop |

Clips are 1152x720 (desktop) or 720x1558 (phone). Phone clips fit the 9:16
frame; desktop clips need a crop or a blurred background bar.
